Nudgy

Things Get Done.

A calm daily planner that helps you get things done without the noise. Plan tasks and reminders in seconds, block focus time, and keep your Google Calendar in sync — in English and Bengali.

Now in closed testing on Google Play

What it does

💬 Plan in seconds

Type it, or just say what you need in the chat — Nudgy turns it into a task, an event, or a focus block, with reminders at the exact time you set.

📅 Calendar in sync

Two-way sync with Google Calendar: events created in Nudgy appear in Google Calendar, and changes there flow back.

🎙️ Voice input

Tap the mic in chat and say what you need — in English or Bengali — and Nudgy turns it into a plan. Nothing listens in the background.

🔒 Your data, your choice

Guest Mode works fully offline, no account needed. Sign in only if you want private sync — and delete your account and data anytime.
