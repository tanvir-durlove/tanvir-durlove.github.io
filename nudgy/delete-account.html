Delete your Nudgy account

You can delete your Nudgy account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1 — Inside the app (fastest)
  1. Open Nudgy and tap Your Nudgy (profile).
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This permanently removes your cloud data (tasks, events, memories, chats, profile) and your local app data.

Option 2 — By email

Send a deletion request from the email address you signed in with to nextgentoolbd@gmail.com. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Guest Mode: guest data never leaves your device. Signing out of Guest Mode removes the guest session; uninstalling the app removes all guest data.
