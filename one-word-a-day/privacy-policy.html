Privacy Policy — One Word A Day

Developer: NextGen Tools BD
Contact: nextgentoolbd@gmail.com
Effective date: October 24, 2026

One Word A Day is a daily 5-letter word puzzle game. Gameplay is fully offline, requires no account, and has no subscription.

Information we collect

The app itself does not collect, store, or share your name, email address, contacts, location, or any other personal information. Your puzzles, guesses, streaks, and statistics stay on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, including the advertising ID, to serve and measure ads. This is the only third-party data collection in the app, and it is used for advertising and marketing purposes only. Data collected by AdMob is encrypted in transit. You can reset your device advertising ID in your Android settings.

Permissions

The app may request notifications permission for the optional daily puzzle reminder, and network access to load ads. No other sensitive permissions are required for gameplay.

Children

The app is rated for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data deletion

Because the app stores your game data only on your device and requires no account, there is no server-side account data to delete. Uninstalling the app removes its on-device data. For questions about AdMob data, please refer to Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
